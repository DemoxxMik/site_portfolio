About

Hello and welcome!

My name is Mikaël Mahussier. I am a Master’s student in digital creation at ESADSE. I like working with animation, scenography and video games to explore emotions and the way they move through us, sometimes gently, sometimes more intensely.

I try to give shape to the feelings we express less often, such as anxiety or certain fears, in order to make them more tangible and easier to grasp.
